An isolated small prehistoric cave-pigment motif on a perfectly solid black background, no rock surface: a shallow chalk-white uneven oval tray enclosing four small unpainted-black pebble circles, joined by a thin curved red ochre tether that returns each pebble to the tray. Meaning: retain the entire planned set, even missing observations. Simple handmade Lascaux mineral pigment, worn stippled white chalk, red and yellow ochre only. Motif centered and occupying central 70 percent of square. No text, letters, numbers, hands, figures, logos, symbols or frame.
